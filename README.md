# Project Overview

This Power BI project provides a comprehensive analysis of India's quick-commerce market by comparing Blinkit and Instamart across multiple business dimensions.

The dashboard helps stakeholders understand:

* Market growth trends
* Profitability performance
* Regional and city-level opportunities
* Product and category contribution
* Strategic expansion priorities
* Revenue impact of dark-store expansion

---

# Business Problem

India's quick-commerce market is rapidly evolving, driven by increasing consumer demand, dark-store expansion, and intense competition.

This project aims to answer key business questions:

* Which company is leading the market?
* How profitable are Blinkit and Instamart?
* Which regions and cities contribute the highest revenue?
* Which categories drive customer demand?
* Where should future expansion be prioritized?
* What is the revenue impact of expanding dark-store networks?

# Key Insights

* Blinkit contributes approximately **69%** of combined NOV.
* Blinkit achieved positive EBITDA in FY26.
* South India contributes the largest share of regional NOV.
* Delhi NCR is the highest-performing city market.
* Grocery & Staples remains the largest revenue-driving category.
* East India presents a long-term expansion opportunity.
* Store productivity plays a critical role in sustainable growth.
